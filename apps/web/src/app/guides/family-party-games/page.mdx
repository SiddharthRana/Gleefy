# Party games for mixed-age family gatherings

Family gatherings bring together people who don’t always share interests, slang or attention spans. The right game gets everyone laughing at the same table. Here’s how to choose.

## Choose games where everyone can win

- **This or That** works for every age: there are no wrong answers, only strong opinions.
- **Would You Rather** gets grandparents and teenagers arguing about the same thing, which is the point.
- **Guess the Answer** rewards different kinds of knowledge, so a younger player can beat an older one.
- **Trivia Blitz** is best in **mixed teams**: pair different generations so each team has a spread of knowledge.

## Pick categories with care

- Every category shows its age guidance and any content note before you play. Gleefy is made for ages 13 and up.
- Around the World and World Food make great conversation starters about trips, family recipes and places you'd love to go.
- If someone feels left out of a category, switch it. You can change categories between games in seconds.

## Make it easy to join in

- **Turn on Big text** in Settings so the reader doesn’t have to squint, and so others can read over a shoulder.
- **Let the most reluctant person be the reader.** Reading out questions is a low-pressure way to join in.
- **Turn timers off** for a relaxed game, or on to stop the family debater from taking over.

## Keep it kind

- Skip any question that touches a sore spot. Families know where those are.
- Celebrate funny answers, not just right ones.
- Stop while people are still enjoying it. They’ll ask to play again next time.
