# Hosting a game night on one phone

You don’t need a console, a board game or everyone downloading an app. One phone and a few people sitting together is enough. Here’s how to make it go smoothly.

## Before people arrive

- **Charge the phone** and turn on Do Not Disturb, so notifications don’t pop up over a question.
- **Add player or team names** in MirthMix. Trivia and Guess the Answer use them for turns and scores, and This or That uses them for “who agreed most”.
- **Pick a few categories** you think will suit the group. You can change them between games.
- **Check the content notes** shown on each category. Skip anything that doesn’t fit the room.

## A running order that works

Start light and build up:

1. **This or That** to warm up. Picks take seconds and nobody can get them wrong.
2. **Would You Rather** once people are talking. Let the minority defend their choice.
3. **Guess the Answer** in teams, for a bit of competition.
4. **Trivia Blitz** to finish, with a winner to celebrate.

You can save that order as a [custom party pack](/games/custom-party-pack) and play it back to back.

## Pacing

- **Keep each game short.** Ten rounds is plenty; fifteen if the room is buzzing.
- **Skip freely.** A question that falls flat isn’t worth the silence; skipping doesn’t use up a round.
- **Use timers to speed things up**, and turn them off when people are enjoying the debate.
- **Rotate the phone.** The reader is part of the fun, so pass that role around.

## House rules that help

- Nobody has to explain an answer they don’t want to.
- Close answers count in Guess the Answer if the group agrees.
- In Trivia, only the team whose turn it is may answer. Others can heckle.

## When things go wrong

- **A call comes in?** Timers pause while the app is in the background, and the game is saved. Come back and carry on.
- **Phone switched off?** MirthMix remembers the game in progress and offers to resume it.
- **Someone joins late?** Add their name before the next game; the current game keeps its line-up.

Have fun, and let the arguments run.
